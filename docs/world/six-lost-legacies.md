# The Six Lost Legacies — abandoned fae villages and optional questlines

**Authored:** October 21, 2026 · **Status:** Comprehensive narrative **proposal**, not approved line-by-line or implemented. **Owner-approved premise:** The fall of the fae is chiefly discovered through optional side quests. The six clans, Elarwyn and the fae's Sylweave-linked plague are established lore.

[Quest framework](quest-framework.md) · [Six fae clans](fae-clans.md) · [Elarwyn](elarwyn-sanctuary.md) · [Ellowyn](ellowyn-and-the-sylweave.md)

## Design promises

- Players may finish Ellowyn's main story without doing any of these six arcs. Main-path evidence of the chemical and how to stop extraction must be available independently.
- Each village has a distinct *human-scale* tragedy; ruins reveal lives, professions, animal care and community, not just corpses and loot.
- Clues come from objects, ecology, architecture, creatures, personal records and occasionally Syltouch; journal text and accessibility alternatives must exist for audiovisual cues.
- Nothing requires players to destroy heritage sites, kill potentially treatable victims, or abandon their animals to progress.
- Each arc ends in a **persistent visible restoration** and gives a useful homesteading, animal-care or exploration reward. Reward power cannot gate main-game completion.
- Proposed encounters, NPCs and assets are **not implemented**, balanced, costed, voiced or tested.

## Discovery and collection structure

**Entry:** An optional Elarwyn archival board lists six unanswered settlement stories. Players can also stumble upon any ruin naturally, without visiting Elarwyn first. Ilyra Aethrynn can help contextualize discoveries, but is not mandatory to visit each time.

**Per arc loop:** find site → read physical clues → meet a survivor, witness or animal trace → reconstruct a difficult decision → resolve a present-day consequence → recover an heirloom or practice → bring findings to Elarwyn or place them in a personal homestead archive. Co-op players can discover evidence together; world restoration persists on the authoritative server and is replay-safe for late joiners.

**Collection finale (optional):** *The Names We Carry*. The Six Trees garden in Elarwyn gains six engraved memory stones. If all six histories are completed, the community conducts a remembrance in which surviving clans share what was lost and what endures. The ceremony **does not cure the plague**, revive the dead or gate the main climax. It offers an optional, meaningful restoration and cosmetic/building patterns.

## 1. Sylvaren — The Last Rootsong

**Site:** **Rootmere Hollow**, once a community built around enormous exposed willow roots deep within an old forest nearer Ellowyn.

**Seen on arrival:** Walkways fused into bark, deserted family balconies, candles melted to stubs around a circular Syltouch chamber. The huge central root pulses irregularly; branches beyond the village remain strangely still. An empty children's learning circle contains carved maps of places no longer reachable through root-memory.

**People:** **Seren Sylvaren**, a distant descendant preserving nursery songs in Elarwyn; **Orlen**, the last caretaker who recorded the final communal listening before the evacuation (heard only through a fractured memory).

**Quest beats:**
1. Find a child's braided root-charm beside the old village path, or accept Seren's request to locate an ancestor's record.
2. Follow successive clues through homes and the teaching grove; locate three **memory leaves**, each showing a different final day: families evacuating, elders debating whether Syltouch is safe, and volunteers choosing to isolate the failing root chamber.
3. Approach the blighted chamber while accompanied by a trusted animal whose reactions help locate stable footing. Optional Syltouch reveals that the root was silenced deliberately to reduce harmful exposure, **not** by a treacherous elder.
4. Secure the chamber without forcing it awake; choose whether to leave a memorial or establish a carefully monitored quiet sanctuary.
5. Bring the names of those who stayed behind to Seren.

**Mystery and tragedy:** Survivors spent centuries believing their elders had abandoned their duty to Ellowyn. In truth, the last listeners severed their own communion to protect the evacuation route.

**Present threat:** Root-sick wildlife and unstable terrain, handled through careful navigation or nonlethal avoidance where possible.

**Lasting result:** A faintly living memorial ring returns to the ruin; in Elarwyn a tree receives a carved name-plaque. **Proposed reward:** living-wood arches, root-pattern benches and a decorative Syltouch garden feature. Not a free source of unlimited Sylweave magic.

## 2. Vaelora — Beneath the Stillwater

**Site:** **Lethwyn Quay**, an elegant river village with canal terraces, floodgates and half-submerged lantern bridges.

**Seen on arrival:** A riverside dining table prepared for an unfinished festival, fishing skiffs tied neatly to an empty dock, floating lanterns caught under a bridge, and strangely stagnant water upstream of an old diversion.

**People:** **Neris Vaelora**, elderly ferrymaker who insists her family was left behind; **Talen**, a boatbuilder whose weather-damaged log survives in a sealed hull.

**Quest beats:**
1. Trace an abandoned postal skiff or hear Neris's story in Elarwyn.
2. Recover navigation markers, a floodgate maintenance diagram and a song-map from three separate points of the village.
3. Learn that villagers redirected a contaminated surge away from a downstream evacuation flotilla, sacrificing their home and perhaps some boats to protect families further downriver.
4. Identify a safer channel using water marks, wildlife movements and engineering records; restore a small bypass rather than opening every floodgate at once.
5. Deliver the names and real sequence of events to Neris; optionally guide a ceremonial skiff along the now-safe reach.

**Mystery and tragedy:** The apparently inexplicable mass departure was an emergency water diversion. Those seen as deserters were rescuers; accounts disagree over who made the final call, preserving ambiguity.

**Present threat:** Unstable dock structures, contaminated pools and territorial wetland fauna; appropriate animal companionship can help scout, not substitute for player agency.

**Lasting result:** A small navigable shore landing, restored lanterns and remembrance plaques. **Proposed reward:** fae bridge styles, waterside railings, skiff fittings or decorative water-garden plans (actual watercraft mechanics unconfirmed).

## 3. Aethrynn — The Stars Remember

**Site:** **High Aster Observatory**, a mountain settlement linked by terraces to an abandoned star-mapping tower and subterranean library.

**Seen on arrival:** An observatory dome halted halfway open, weathered charts, pale stone residences, a preserved night garden and an archive door intentionally jammed from the inside.

**People:** **Ilyra Aethrynn** of Elarwyn; **Master Envar**, historical keeper of the observatory whose notes survive in fractured notebooks.

**Quest beats:**
1. Ilyra asks for a clan map that might reveal when the first human Weirs appeared; or players find the observatory by exploring peaks.
2. Repair simple astronomical instruments by aligning engraved sky positions, with an accessible journal diagram.
3. Compare three contradictory timelines: old fae root-records, Weir-builder inscriptions and the observatory's measurements of unusual Sylweave fluctuations.
4. Unseal the archive and discover that Aethrynn scholars **recognized the chemical danger early**, but their warnings were delayed by political arguments and incomplete evidence—not a single omniscient prophecy.
5. Safeguard copies for the public archive; optionally preserve the originals at the mountain site.

**Mystery and tragedy:** Scholars tried to prove a danger while the realm demanded certainty. The delay cost lives; their records can finally be understood without turning the entire clan into cowards or sages.

**Present threat:** Thin ledges, damaged lifting equipment, nesting highland animals and possible Veyrath scavengers seeking the same records.

**Lasting result:** A functioning observation lantern and reopened archive gallery. **Proposed reward:** pale-stone building kits, observatory furnishings, sky-map journal pages and decorative navigation pieces.

## 4. Veltharyn — The Sealed Court

**Site:** **The Silver Court of Veylspire**, an austere manor-village hidden among dark-leaved woods, with gates closed from within.

**Seen on arrival:** Carefully maintained but empty banquet tables, heraldic knots, shuttered balconies and tiny offerings outside the doors from later pilgrims. The sealed gates look deliberate, not besieged.

**People:** **Vaelor Veltharyn**, Elarwyn's guarded oathkeeper; **Arelis**, former gate-warden known through written testimony; an elderly caretaker living beyond the boundary could be a rare survivor.

**Quest beats:**
1. Receive a faded visitor oath from Vaelor or encounter the locked court and find its public inscription.
2. Recover sealed testimony from an outside watch-house, the servants' hall and a greenhouse used as a temporary isolation ward.
3. Discover a divide: open the gates to help the ill, or quarantine the court to prevent a wider catastrophe. Leaders chose quarantine, while several servants secretly organized a route for uninfected children.
4. Open a **safe outer memorial passage** after evaluating surviving signs of contamination; never assume that breaking every seal is heroic.
5. Share the complete evidence with living descendants, allowing them to recognize both sacrifices and abuses of authority.

**Mystery and tragedy:** The court's secrecy protected some lives but concealed others' suffering. The story resists a simple answer about whether isolation was right.

**Present threat:** Failing wards, predatory creatures sheltering in deserted wings, and contaminated interior chambers; choice of access should reflect precaution.

**Lasting result:** Public memorial walk and preserved quarantine records, without automatically reopening dangerous wings. **Proposed reward:** silver latticework, elegant robes, oath-seal motifs and furniture patterns; no coercive mind-control skill.

## 5. Thornwyld — The Beasts That Wait

**Site:** **Brackenwatch Fold**, a woodland settlement of animal yards, grazing terraces and tree-built lodges.

**Seen on arrival:** Empty feeding sheds, worn wooden nameboards, quiet pasture bells and herds returning to gates long after their handlers disappeared. A huge old guardian creature still patrols the boundary, agitated rather than intrinsically evil.

**People:** **Thessa Thornwyld**, Beastwarden in Elarwyn; **Berren**, a former stable apprentice whose sketchbook records every animal once housed here.

**Quest beats:**
1. Hear of the ghostly herd or accept Thessa's request to investigate missing bloodlines and caretaker tools.
2. Follow tracks, animal calls, pasture damage and feed records instead of only fighting through the site.
3. Learn that caretakers stayed behind to move animals to safer habitats and that certain trained guardians inherited routines from their vanished companions.
4. Earn the elder guardian's trust through observations, safe distance, appropriate feed and restoration of broken enclosures; hunting or attacking it is never mandatory.
5. Reopen a protected animal resting area and add the caretakers' names to Elarwyn's memorial.

**Mystery and tragedy:** The animals' continued presence isn't a curse; it is a living legacy of relationships and training that persisted after the fae left.

**Present threat:** Stress, sickness and territorial defense among abandoned livestock and wildlife, plus physically damaged fences.

**Lasting result:** A functioning wildlife waystation. **Proposed reward:** clan-specific stalls, humane enclosures, bells, tack cosmetics and a new animal-care interaction once prototyped; avoid an overpowered automatic tame.

## 6. Elarith — The Healer's Folly

**Site:** **Dawnspring Infirmary**, a beautiful clinic-village surrounded by medicinal terraces, abandoned greenhouses and isolation cottages.

**Seen on arrival:** Hundreds of plant labels, meticulously numbered remedies, untouched beds and a garden where one disease-resistant plant variety still grows next to poisoned soil.

**People:** **Caelen Elarith**, plague scholar; **Healer Aranya**, remembered through annotated patient records; a surviving adult who was treated as a child can provide a personal counterpoint.

**Quest beats:**
1. Caelen seeks the lost early treatment ledger, or the player discovers an incomplete plant inventory.
2. Collect several documented medicinal ingredients **without** assuming these plants can cure a chemically sourced Sylweave illness.
3. Compare dosage notes with patient outcomes and discover that one apparent miracle only masked symptoms while exposure continued.
4. Secure hazardous remnants of a failed experiment, preserving information about why it failed and the ethics of treatment consent.
5. Return the records to Caelen, who accepts that healing patients matters but stopping the chemical source is necessary to prevent further disease.

**Mystery and tragedy:** The healers were neither villains nor miracle-workers; they made costly mistakes, corrected what they could and cared for patients even when cure was beyond their reach.

**Present threat:** Unsafe chemical residues, brittle glasshouses and diseased roots. Avoid gameplay that asks players to manufacture or spread the Veyrath's dangerous reagent.

**Lasting result:** Cleaned memorial herb terrace and restored archive of careful treatments. **Proposed reward:** fae greenhouse building set, medicinal planter variants and non-magical recuperation furnishings; any actual health bonuses would need game balancing.

## Optional finale — The Names We Carry

**Trigger:** All six memories have been recovered, independent of main-story chapter progress. Elarwyn's council offers a remembrance rather than a compulsory task.

**Ceremony:** Six engraved memory stones stand among the Six Trees. Descendants sing fragments of songs and recount the choices of those who remained in the old villages. Animal companions may be invited to rest quietly at the outer garden. One tree's living response precedes further hopeful signs, without making six completed side quests equal an instant Sylweave cure.

**Rewards (proposed):** A decorative six-clan memorial arch, a communal journal containing the recorded legacies, expanded furniture patterns, and acknowledgement in dialogue. All are optional. If Ellowyn has already been saved, the ceremony acknowledges the hard recovery still ahead; if not, it reaffirms the need to stop the Veyrath.

## Dependency and production notes

**Small proof first:** Prototype one abandoned micro-settlement, three discoverable clues, one noncombat animal interaction and one persistent visible change. Test two-player dedicated-server replication, late-joining party journal state, persistence across restart and backup/rollback before multiplying by six.

**Content later:** Each finished arc requires environment art, sound, journal UX, scripting, interaction rules, accessibility handling and server-authoritative quest-state design. Story text and optional rewards here do **not** imply any features are implemented or tested.
