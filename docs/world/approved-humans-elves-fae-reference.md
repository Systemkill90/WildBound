# Approved reference — Humans, Elves and Fae

**Owner approval:** October 22, 2026. **Status:** approved concept-art benchmark only. This document records the exact **six-person group portrait** generated in the Wildbound chat: male and female human on the left, male and female elf in the middle, male and female fae on the right. The image is preserved as `Wildbound_Approved_Humans_Elves_Fae.png` in the conversation download; **not yet uploaded as a binary to GitHub**.

## Humans
- Human-sized, plainly attractive at most, ordinary and weathered features. Rustic, utilitarian, patched and layered clothing with muted browns and greys; no visual magic.
- Intentionally unglamorous, practical, credible everyday villagers and travelers.

## Elves
- Approximately human height; graceful, agile, long-lived and more attractive than humans without the godlike fae appearance.
- **Long, mostly straight hair**, subtle small **upright** pointed ears **close to the head**, **no tattoos or natural magic**.
- Clothing is one refinement above human dress: elegant, fitted, functional, subtle embroidered woodland motifs.

## Fae
- **All over 6 feet 5 inches, including women**, notably taller than humans and elves.
- Strong, lean, muscular adult physiques; sharp angular facial structure and almost godlike attractiveness.
- **Large outward-pointing pointed ears**, intricate extensive **tribal tattoos**, richly detailed elegant clothing.
- **Translucent, butterfly/fairy-like wings** with fine gold-veined iridescence, **not feathered wings**.
- Naturally magical and capable of Syltouch; flight gameplay remains undecided.

## Image composition and continuity
- Three clearly separate pairs, left to right: humans, elves, fae; proportions visibly different in the same grounded cinematic fantasy setting.
- The approved portrait shows both genders for each species, six total.
- Reuse this exact image and its character proportions in later all-species composites; avoid regressing fae to childlike fairies or giving elves oversized sideways ears.
- **Artwork only:** not finished meshes, textures, animation rigs or UE5-tested assets.
