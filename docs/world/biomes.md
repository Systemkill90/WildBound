# World and biome concepts

[Vision](../vision.md) · [Animal life](../systems/animals.md)

All biomes are planned. Species are candidates, not a committed roster. The first prototype should use a small Greenveil test area.

## Greenveil Basin — a home worth tending

**Mood:** cottagecore, pastoral, whimsical, welcoming but untamed.

Rolling meadows, birch groves, orchards, creek crossings, flower banks, and enormous mossy trees frame the early homestead fantasy. Timber cottages, stone garden walls, barns, sheepfolds, apiaries, and small bridges belong here. Rain, mud, grazing pressure, and foxlike scavengers maintain ecological credibility.

Candidate wildlife includes deer, rabbits, foxes, meadow birds, and large grazing herbivores. Chickens, sheep, goats, and cattle are candidate livestock. Glowing moths, mushroom rings, hollow ancient trees, and springs surrounded by unusually vigorous plants introduce quiet magic. Effects should be observed before they are explained.

**Design role:** learn gathering, building, growing food, animal care, and cooperative settlement. Cozy still leaves room for danger.

## Greenveil's permanent season — approved October 24, 2026

**Owner decision:** Greenveil Basin stays **eternally green, lush and springlike**. Its trees do **not** turn autumn colors and do **not** undergo seasonal leaf fall. Keep thriving green foliage, flowers, greenery, meadows and verdant forests as the region's distinctive year-round appearance. Ordinary daily weather and lighting can still vary; do not infer a required four-season visual cycle for this biome.

A **separate fall/autumn biome elsewhere in Wildbound** will provide autumn colors and atmosphere. Its name, geography, inhabitants and design are **not decided**. Do not reclassify an established biome or approve an exploratory biome name without owner approval.

Earlier suggestions about seasonal flower blooms or seasonal fishing changes **are superseded for Greenveil** by this simplified permanent-green direction; specific flower and fish populations remain to be designed separately.

## Greenveil Basin — expanded fantasy geography (owner direction, October 23, 2026)

**Confirmed direction:** Greenveil should be a **sprawling rolling countryside**, with extensive forests, lakes and networks of **smaller valleys**, not one single broad mountain-enclosed bowl. Give the pastoral setting a **more overt fantasy atmosphere** while retaining ecology and believable materials. Include **small villages belonging to Kethri, Nimrui and Wolfkin**, reflecting each people's distinct identity and scale. They are allied or friendly in the broader established relationship map, although individual settlements retain their own cultures.

**Proposed visual language (not yet locked):**
- Meadow hills, flowering hedgerows, picturesque lake shores, sheltered valley hamlets, wooded ridges, old-growth canopies and enormous mossy boulders; sweeping vista transitions into intimate pathways and secluded settlements.
- Woodland Kethri canopy dwellings with elevated walkways, balconies and bridges; keep the inhabitants small and adult-looking.
- Nimrui fungal-garden hollows with mushroom-shaped shelter forms and luminous gardens; keep the inhabitants very small, dark-skinned and distinct from Kethri.
- Wolfkin timber-and-stone communities by forest edges, lake shores and hills: sturdier communal halls, workspaces and wide circulation appropriate to their tall, powerfully built wolf-like anatomy.
- Ancient standing stones, forgotten paths, Sylweave-fed springs, quietly luminous flora, uncommon plants and soft phenomena at dusk. Use enchantment to enrich the landscape without making everything glow.
- Maintain meaningful farms, livestock, weather, roads and animal habitat; balance welcoming homestead spaces with wild areas and the distant threat of poisoning.

The settlement categories and architecture above are **proposals for further owner review**, not final names, placement coordinates, built assets or functioning gameplay. The approved player-character and species visual reference sheets remain authoritative for anatomy. Start by prototyping one modest, explorable portion of Greenveil and its vistas before attempting the full region.

## The Drowned March — the water remembers

**Mood:** spooky, mournful, ancient, and watchful.

Blackwater pools, pale reeds, twisted wetland trees, hanging moss, peat banks, and half-sunken causeways disappear into low fog. Ruined statues and rotten boardwalks suggest a lost human presence. Distant splashes and cries make the player uneasy even when nothing attacks.

Wading birds, amphibians, aquatic grazers, and elusive wetland predators are candidate ecological roles. Stilt houses, reed roofs, watch platforms, raised walkways, and elevated pens suit the terrain. Uncertain footing, exposure, and limited visibility provide grounded hazards.

Wisp-like lights, night-blooming bog flowers, preserved artifacts, and luminous fungus create environmental magical realism. Supernatural explanations remain ambiguous. Original wetland folklore, wildlife, and drowned ruins should carry the fear rather than constant overt ghosts.

**Design role:** cautious exploration, rare wetland materials, and ecological mysteries.

## Emberstep Highlands — room to roam

**Mood:** golden, windswept, rugged frontier.

Tall grasses, red stone outcrops, exposed ridges, and broad skies support a future ranching landscape. Herds and predators make open-range management meaningful. Corrals, long barns, and stone windbreaks suit the region. Standing stones that sing in certain winds provide a restrained magical accent.

**Design role:** future herding and longer expeditions; outside the first prototype.

## World rules

- Resources, animal adaptations, weather, shelter, and architecture reinforce ecology.
- Magic alters familiar patterns sparingly, preserving mystery.
- Emotional identity matters as much as resource distribution.
- Test travel and biome boundaries before expanding the world.
