# World and biome concepts

[Vision](../vision.md) · [Animal life](../systems/animals.md)

**Status (October 22, 2026):** a populated 180 × 160 m Greenveil exploration zone has been compiled, packaged and traversed: forest, creek, bridges, cottage placeholders and connected trails. [Test evidence](../project/GREENVEIL-EXPLORATION-2026-10-22.md). No animal gameplay is active in this map. The full region and other production biomes remain design concepts; habitat/species examples below are candidates, not a locked roster.

## Greenveil Basin — a home worth tending

**Mood:** cottagecore, pastoral, whimsical, welcoming but untamed.

Rolling meadows, birch groves, orchards, creek crossings, flower banks, and enormous mossy trees frame the early homestead fantasy. Timber cottages, stone garden walls, barns, sheepfolds, apiaries, and small bridges belong here. Rain, mud, grazing pressure, and foxlike scavengers maintain ecological credibility.

Candidate wildlife includes deer, rabbits, foxes, meadow birds, and large grazing herbivores. Chickens, sheep, goats, and cattle are candidate livestock. Glowing moths, mushroom rings, hollow ancient trees, and springs surrounded by unusually vigorous plants introduce quiet magic. Effects should be observed before they are explained.

**Design role:** learn gathering, building, growing food, animal care, and cooperative settlement. Cozy still leaves room for danger.

**First test grounds:** Greenveil begins at a compact starter village, not in a large empty clearing. See the [starter village slice brief](greenveil-starter-village.md) for the arrival green, home/workshop lane, farm edge, creek crossing, forest loop, and verification order. The village is the visual and movement test environment; animal gameplay remains deferred.

## The Drowned March — the water remembers

**Mood:** spooky, mournful, ancient, and watchful.

Blackwater pools, pale reeds, twisted wetland trees, hanging moss, peat banks, and half-sunken causeways disappear into low fog. Ruined statues and rotten boardwalks suggest a lost human presence. Distant splashes and cries make the player uneasy even when nothing attacks.

Wading birds, amphibians, aquatic grazers, and elusive wetland predators are candidate ecological roles. Stilt houses, reed roofs, watch platforms, raised walkways, and elevated pens suit the terrain. Uncertain footing, exposure, and limited visibility provide grounded hazards.

Wisp-like lights, night-blooming bog flowers, preserved artifacts, and luminous fungus create environmental magical realism. Supernatural explanations remain ambiguous. Original wetland folklore, wildlife, and drowned ruins should carry the fear rather than constant overt ghosts.

**Design role:** cautious exploration, rare wetland materials, and ecological mysteries.

## Emberstep Highlands — room to roam

**Mood:** golden, windswept, rugged frontier.

Tall grasses, red stone outcrops, exposed ridges, and broad skies support a future ranching landscape. Herds and predators make open-range management meaningful. Corrals, long barns, and stone windbreaks suit the region. Standing stones that sing in certain winds provide a restrained magical accent.

**Design role:** future herding and longer expeditions; outside the first prototype.

## World rules

- Resources, animal adaptations, weather, shelter, and architecture reinforce ecology.
- Magic alters familiar patterns sparingly, preserving mystery.
- Emotional identity matters as much as resource distribution.
- Test travel and biome boundaries before expanding the world.
