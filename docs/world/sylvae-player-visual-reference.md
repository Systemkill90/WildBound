# Approved Sylvae Player Character Visual Reference

**Owner approval:** October 22, 2026. **Status:** approved concept-art direction only; no game-ready models or tested customization.

The owner approved the **latest male-and-female Sylvae reference sheet**, specifically the revision featuring **large catlike eyes with narrow vertical slit pupils**. The generated image is preserved in the conversation as `sylvae_elven_character_design_sheet.png`; **this document does not upload the image binary to GitHub**.

## Approved design
- **Playable people:** Sylvae, Ellowyn's recreated ancient species, not fae. More of Ellowyn's living essence/DNA than any being created since the Ancients; ultimate potential stronger than fae.
- **Body scale:** shorter than human-sized elves, taller than dwarves; compact, athletic and solidly built rather than enormous or frail.
- **Faces:** humanoid, refined and faintly ancient, with subtly fae-like facial structure; intentionally **simple enough for extensive player character customization**.
- **Eyes:** noticeably **large, feline/catlike eyes with distinct vertical slit pupils**; this is the defining facial feature, not merely a yellow iris.
- **Ears:** pointed, size and angle midway between elves' small upright ears and fae's longer outward-pointing ears.
- **Wings:** none. Do not add tree antlers, bark skin, excessive plant growth or mandatory decorative surface detail; those belonged to a discarded exploration and would interfere with player customization.
- **Clothing:** grounded, ancient **Viking-inspired** woven cloth, leather, fur and practical layered equipment. Keep adornments and tattoo-like marks optional or minimal, not hardwired into the body.
- **Magic:** light **or** shadow, **not** the fae's elemental magic. Either alignment is not a moral verdict.

## Continuity
Treat the latest revised concept image as the look benchmark for male and female Sylvae. Future body, skin, face, hair and clothing variation should preserve species recognizability while allowing players individuality. The text printed on AI concept sheets is not authoritative lore if it conflicts with established Wildbound decisions.

No UE5 character rig, creation UI, equipment system, abilities or replication has been validated by this visual approval.
