# Approved Mermaid Visual Reference

**Approved by owner:** October 22, 2026. **Status:** approved visual concept only, not an implemented UE5 character or 3D asset.

The latest **MERMAIDS** character-design poster, following the request to add **more scales across their upper bodies**, is the authoritative visual reference. It was generated in the Wildbound conversation. **This document does not upload the image binary to GitHub.**

## Approved appearance
- Distinct from Brinefolk, yet visibly related in aquatic heritage and refined, **fae-like** facial structure.
- Soft, expressive, relatively humanlike facial features rather than heavily fishlike faces.
- **Large eyes**, with pink, violet, blue or amber variations.
- More extensive, delicate, **iridescent scales across shoulders, arms, upper torso and parts of the face**, blending into fully scaled fish tails.
- Flowing hair and natural shell, pearl and ocean-worn ornaments.
- Distinctive fin shapes and long expressive tails instead of legs; varied pearlescent, coral, teal, violet and dusk-colored finishes.
- Photorealistic fantasy character style in rocky coast and underwater settings.

## Continuity
Retain the **latest upper-body scaling** when creating future mermaid portraits. Do not revert to the earlier near-scaleless upper body or to the Brinefolk's more strongly aquatic facial anatomy. The specific taxonomic relationship to Brinefolk has not been finally decided by the owner; prior generated image text describing mermaids as a “subspecies” is not canon by itself.

Approved **concept-art reference** does not imply meshes, rigs, underwater locomotion, combat or gameplay are finished.
