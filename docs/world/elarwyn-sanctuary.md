# Elarwyn — The Last Fae Sanctuary

**Status:** Owner-approved narrative direction, October 21, 2026. This is **design lore**, not implemented gameplay. Precise map coordinates, timelines, character dialogue, mechanics and quests remain to be built and validated.

## The sanctuary

**Elarwyn** is the major surviving fae settlement: a remote, hidden **mountain-valley refuge** far from Ellowyn, intended to spare its residents further exposure to the plague and permit continued research toward saving her. It belongs to survivors of **all six fae clans**, not one exclusive house. Framed by mountains, waterfalls, woodland, cobbled paths and pale stone bridges, its homes, healing gardens, libraries, glass conservatories and Syltouch groves combine different clan building traditions.

Elarwyn is beautiful and inhabited but visibly underpopulated: empty houses, dark windows, improvised infirmaries, memorial gardens and the strain of ongoing illness reveal the catastrophe. It remains a lived-in place with families, craftspeople, caretakers and animals, not just a hub of quest vendors. Players can return, build relationships, trade and contribute to restoration after the campaign.

## Government — Council of Six

A **Council of Six** governs Elarwyn, one representative from each clan. Each has a distinct priority:
- **Sylvaren / Rootkeepers:** Reestablish communion with Ellowyn before the connection is lost.
- **Vaelora / Riverbound:** Keep water safe and preserve routes and supply.
- **Aethrynn / Starwardens:** Recover records of the first human Weirs and the plague.
- **Veltharyn / Veilkeepers:** Maintain secrecy and protect the refuge from the Veyrath.
- **Thornwyld / Beastbound:** Rescue displaced fae and animals from afflicted regions.
- **Elarith / Dawnhealers:** Study the fae-specific illness and find a treatment.

The council's disputes give players meaningful perspectives; members need not be villains for disagreeing. Exact council membership and voting procedures are unresolved.

## Key characters — owner-approved concept names and roles

- **Maerith Sylvaren — The Last Listener:** Extremely old Syltouch master and spiritual mentor. Contact with injured roots is painful; she remembers the earliest symptoms of Ellowyn's illness.
- **Caelen Elarith — The Plague Scholar:** Brilliant, sometimes arrogant healer researching the mechanism that bypasses fae regenerative resilience.
- **Thessa Thornwyld — The Beastwarden:** Animal guardian and advanced companion-training mentor who helped evacuate wildlife and injured creatures.
- **Vaelor Veltharyn — The Oathkeeper:** Cautious diplomat who fears revealing Elarwyn's location to the Veyrath and knows unsettling history.
- **Ilyra Aethrynn — The Memorykeeper:** Curious fae historian who investigates abandoned settlements, the old human Weirs and clues to the chemical's spread.

Personality, final appearance, relationships, branching dialogue, romance and individual quests remain to be written. These five are important NPCs; they are not automatically all six council delegates.

## Core mystery

The fae know Ellowyn is suffering but have misunderstood why their regenerative gifts are failing. The plague travels through their deep Sylweave connection, yet **the Veyrath's extraction chemistry is poisoning the roots at the source**. The Veyrath's futile search for a clean batch perpetuates the contamination. The sanctuary's research must uncover this causal loop; medicine alone cannot end exposure. The precise disease biology and potential treatment remain open.

Long ago, Syltouch carried memories and signals across vast distances. The sickness has progressively silenced those links. A player bearing Ellowyn's last gift may briefly restore a long-lost connection in Maerith's presence.

**Optional history rule:** Discovering the detailed fall of each fae clan belongs to optional side quests in abandoned villages. The main campaign can use a single necessary piece of evidence from a ruin, but must not require completing the six historical side-quest arcs.

## Campaign chapter — The Silence Between Roots

Proposed quest sequence, accepted as narrative direction, not scripted or implemented:
1. Find a fae guide and the mountain approach to Elarwyn.
2. Win entry to the refuge and meet the divided Council of Six.
3. Reveal that Ellowyn's gift enables a connection the fae have lost.
4. Help the healers examine contaminated roots and retrieve records from an abandoned fae settlement.
5. Demonstrate that the Veyrath's extraction process is continuously feeding the plague.
6. Persuade the council to support an expedition toward Ellowyn.

Potential rewards: Syltouch learning, fae building designs, medicinal garden craft, unique companion-care knowledge and enduring alliances; exact gameplay effects are **unapproved** until designed and tested.

## The Six Trees

Every evening, survivors may gather at a memorial garden of **six great trees**, one for each clan. Their roots once enabled communal sharing of memories through Syltouch. **Only one still responds.** Following meaningful restoration, a second tree begins to glow, visibly showing that life and connection are returning. This is a strong environmental storytelling beat, not an implemented cutscene.

## Production boundary

No fae village level, NPC, council dialogue, playable quest, Syltouch functionality, or plague systems are implemented or tested as part of this lore approval. Begin eventually with one small NPC encounter and one root-memory scene before committing to a full sanctuary and political simulation.
