## Approved visual identity
- Greenveil stays eternally green, lush, verdant and springlike. No autumn foliage and no seasonal leaf drop. The future fall biome is separate and remains unnamed.
- Realistic, high-fidelity UE5 game-environment aesthetic; magical fantasy atmosphere over plausible ecology, terrain, weather, and settlement construction.
- Sprawling rolling countryside, smaller connected valleys, freshwater lakes, streams, rivers, waterfalls, forested ridges, winding paths, bridges, orchards and homestead-friendly meadows.
- Deep ancient forests with broadleaf oak, birch, beech/hornbeam and waterside willow; ferns, moss, bluebells, wildflowers, reeds, water lilies and selective luminous fungal accents. These plant families are approved *as depicted in the board*; exact botanical distribution and technical asset choices remain to be defined.
- Kethri: small-scale woodland canopy villages integrating elevated homes, footbridges and enormous living trees.
- Nimrui: small-scale sheltered mushroom hollows near streams, with giant fungi, cozy homes and restrained magical glow.
- Wolfkin: lakefront timber-and-stone communities with broad communal halls, piers and rugged construction suited to the people's stature.
- Freshwater Brinefolk: lake-dwelling communities in the Great Lake District, potentially using reeds, sheltered coves, stilted structures and semi-submerged architecture; exact structures are still design proposals.
- Humans who have rejected or left Veyrath: modest independent refuge settlements and farms in Greenveil, distinct from hostile Veyrath holdings. Their exact sites and names remain open.
- Elves: elegant carved timber and pale-stone settlements in the forests, especially ancient forest clearings, with bridges, gardens and living-tree integration.
- Wildlings: distinct, deeply forest-integrated sanctuaries in the ancient forests, using bark, roots, natural shelters and concealed paths; preserve their approved antlered, dark-skinned, green-eyed, white-tattooed identity. They are allied with elves.
- Ancient mossy ruins, unusually old trees, Sylweave landmarks and naturally mysterious spaces; not everything should glow.
- Bright days, misty mornings, golden sunsets, moonlit nights and rain/storm changes without seasonal landscape recoloring.

## Boundaries
The reference image is a **composition and fidelity target**; it is not a literal engineering blueprint or an approval of every tiny artifact or unreviewed structure in generated art. In particular, do not infer a mandatory giant castle, floating island, snowfield inside Greenveil or mountain-enclosed basin. Preserve approved species anatomy, lore, village identities and the rolling-countryside geography.
The page locks artistic direction, not world size, precise village placements, terrain heightfields, creature AI, named structures or performance claims. Do not treat concept art as Unreal meshes or licensed game assets.
