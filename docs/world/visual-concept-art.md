## Art direction

- Keep real, prehistoric, and fantastical species grounded in the same believable habitats.
- Make creature size, anatomy, movement, and distance readable at a glance.
- Let coexistence, tracks, feeding, shelter, and seasonal behavior carry the worldbuilding.
- Use magic sparingly; retain the familiar wilderness atmosphere before revealing the strange.
- Treat every species above as a candidate until gameplay, ecological fit, and production scope are tested.

Generated imagery is concept reference only. Production characters, environments, and game assets will need their own art direction, licensing review, and implementation.
