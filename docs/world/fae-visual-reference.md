# Approved Fae Visual Reference

**Approved by owner:** October 22, 2026. **Status:** definitive concept-art direction, not implemented character assets.

The owner approved the most recent **male and female fae portrait set in ancient overgrown stone ruins**, after changing **only** the original dark featherlike wings into **large, translucent, luminous fairy-like wings with delicate gold-veined patterns and iridescence**. The actual generated image remains attached to the conversation; this text file does not upload the image bytes into GitHub.

## Exact appearance to preserve

- All adult fae, including women, are **over 6 feet 5 inches tall**.
- Lean, exceptionally strong, muscular builds with defined athletic anatomy; emphatically **adult**, not childlike or petite.
- Almost **godlike physical beauty** with prominent cheekbones, very sharp jawlines, and strikingly refined faces.
- Dramatically pointed ears extending outward from the head (unlike elves' small upright ears).
- Elaborate, extensive **tribal-style dark tattoos** visible across shoulders, arms, torsos and legs.
- **Large translucent fairy/insect-like wings** growing from their backs, with fine sparkling gold-veined tracery; **not feathered bird or angel wings**. Wings may fold/tuck for clothing and environments; working flight remains undecided.
- Luxurious but rustic high-fantasy long garments and leather, dark textiles, pale fabric, fine metallic accessories, with characteristic Woodland magical realism.
- Original composition shows adult dark-haired male and pale-haired female, standing amid ancient stone ruins, each displaying the approved wing and tattoo treatment.

## Lore and implementation separation

Fae use magic and **Syltouch**; elves do not have inherent magic. Fae have exceptional healing, long but finite lives, and can succumb to fae-specific disease associated with Ellowyn's chemical blight. They remain a separate NPC people from the playable Sylvae. The approved image is a **visual reference**, not a rigged model, costume pack, Unreal character, or gameplay-tested asset.

**Important continuity:** When generating more fae designs, preserve this physique, face, ears, tribal markings and wing translucency. Do not revert to small childlike fairies or feathered angel wings.
