# Great Lake District — environment design draft 01

**Date:** October 25, 2026
**Status:** DESIGN PROPOSALS pending owner review, with approved canon explicitly identified.
**Parent:** [Greenveil regional atlas](greenveil-regional-master-map.md), [settlement roster](greenveil-settlement-concepts.md), [ground layer](greenveil-ground-layer-approved.md).

## 1. Non-negotiable approved canon
- Greenveil eternally lush and spring-green, without autumn leaves or seasonal snow. Daily weather, time of day, rain, fog, storms may vary.
- Sprawling rolling countryside/old woods/smaller valleys and interconnected freshwater lakes, streams and rivers.
- Wolfkin in lake-edge timber/stone settlements; **freshwater Brinefolk** in Great Lake District shores and water; modest lakeshore humans possible as independent ex-Veyrath communities. Kethri, Nimrui, elves and Wildlings remain part of Greenveil, but all seven cultures should not be crammed into a single lakeside village.
- Brinefolk visual canon: pale luminous pearl skin with scales, fins/webbing, large pink/violet eyes; no redesign or presumption of seawater-only physiology.
- Wolfkin appearance canon: robust strongly wolf-like adult features, wolf ears and tails, fur, no tattoos.
- Sylweave is life current from Ellowyn; avoid ubiquitous glow or unwarranted magic technologies.
- All newly illustrated lake/river/settlement proper names are provisional: Willowmere, Silvermere, Reedmere, Threadwater, Brightrun, Timberfang Hall, Reedwhisper Cove, Oarshade, etc. Ellowyn is cloud-high sacred willow, and not automatically located inside this lake district.
- No new animal gameplay implementation until explicit approval.

## 2. Approved regional map relationship; proposed physical hydrology

**Map flow concept:** northern ridge springs and forest brooks -> northern smaller lake -> central main freshwater lake -> southern outflow through Rolling Heartlands. Eastern sheltered reed lake/coves connect to central waters through a narrow waterway, rather than forming impossible disconnected water levels. Other northern/western tributaries join at realistic low points.

**Physical rules (proposed):**
- Establish a single consistent freshwater datum for connected lakes or a visibly graded stream/drop between unequal lake levels.
- At each river bend account for eroded outer-bank cut, depositional gravel/silt inner bank; waterfalls require a resistant rock lip and receiving plunge pool.
- Northern slopes feed runoff into wetlands, springs and rivulets; no upstream flow without a clearly fantasy source explicitly approved.
- Main lake bathymetry: beach shelves, shallow reed zones, transition slopes and a few deep basins. Do not set exact meter depths without surveyed heightfield/testing and gameplay visibility constraints.
- Protect shallow cove habitat with gentle wave exposure; reserve rocky exposed headlands and sheltered calm marshy edges based on wind/fetch and slope.
- River outflow should actually descend through Heartlands; bridge locations need floodplain and dry approaches.
- Avoid separating the first 512 m test field from the regional hydrology by pretending it is a full-scale regional terrain crop.

## 3. Shoreline kit proposals

| Type | Substrate and vegetation | Structures/uses | Interaction restrictions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open meadow shore | grass to compact damp soil to gravel; clover, flowers, reeds in pockets | safe lake entry view, homes, paths | allow traversable approaches; don't imply swimming gameplay |
| Old willow shore | layered moss, willow roots, shaded mud, overhanging green curtains | quiet scenic walk, Sylweave storytelling | preserve Ellowyn's unique scale |
| Reed cove | fine silt, sedges, lily pads, scattered rock shelves, submerged vegetation | freshwater Brinefolk homes/contact docks | shallow access routes, avoid obstructive reeds |
| Rocky headland | worn stone and boulders, moss and fern cracks, higher dry ledges | Wolfkin halls, overlooks, piers | walkable paths and bridge/collision audit |
| Forest inlet | dark soils, exposed roots, sheltered water, drift logs | tucked-away discoveries | limited sightlines, deliberate safe route |

Use transition strips rather than visually discontinuous material islands. No fixed seasonal color changes.

## 4. Underwater ecological depth bands (design, not fish gameplay)

- **Sunlit shallows:** submerged grasses, pondweeds, stones, sandy/silty lakebed, wave caustics if affordable; small schooling fish *visual candidates only*.
- **Vegetated littoral shelves:** lily roots, aquatic stems, freshwater mussel/shell habitat and shadowed logs; continuity with reed coves and shore ecology.
- **Sloping middle basin:** lower vegetation density, silt/gravel transition, fallen branches, dimmer light; exploration readability over excessive fog.
- **Deep cold basin:** fewer plants, rock faces, rooted archaeological remnants as optional landmarks, light scattering kept physically plausible and restrained.
- **River mouth/flow channel:** gravel bars, smoother cobbles, sediment plume after storms, high-oxygen water species where suitable.
No fish roster, trapping rules, swimming/diving, boat operation, underwater player camera or oxygen mechanic is approved/implemented by this design document. If species are later selected, ecological suitability and player fishing loops require separate design and acceptance.

## 5. Freshwater Brinefolk settlement concept (proposed)

**Working location:** eastern protected cove / reed lake. **Working label:** Reedwhisper Cove, not canon.
- Spatial zones: 1) dry-bank meeting/trade platform accessible to land visitors; 2) reed-roofed stilt walkways/lantern-lit waterside dwellings; 3) submerged stone-shelf structures for aquatic residents; 4) quiet vegetated water channels; 5) optional secluded sacred-water pocket.
- Construction language: smoothly water-worn stone, woven reeds, polished driftwood, freshwater shells, river pebbles and muted pearl/green glass-like accents. Avoid arbitrary tropical coral architecture or futuristic underwater domes.
- Settlement needs: landing decks above variable waterline, shallow approaches without forced swimming, pedestrian scale for land visitors, privacy around aquatic dwellings, paths that do not damage shoreline vegetation.
- Culture/relations: independent Brinefolk identity and cautious practical commerce with others; do **not** assume full Brinefolk–Wolfkin alliance or mandated mixed village.
- No NPCs, interiors, swimming mechanics, underwater building mechanics or quests are implemented.

## 6. Wolfkin shoreline settlement concept (proposed)

**Working location:** northwestern main-lake shore. **Working label:** Timberfang Hall, not canon.
- Communal hall on firm, slightly raised terrain with commanding view, broad timber buildings, stone plinths, practical lodges, craft shelters and protected food/storage yards.
- Working dock and boat sheds on suitable deep enough waterfront; piers tolerate surface-level changes, shore tracks link forest and hall.
- Paths, stairs and doorways scaled to muscular roughly six-foot wolf-like people; inviting protective feel, not automatically a military fortress.
- Material kit: thick weathered logs, chiseled stone, mossy roofing details, carved woodwork without tattoos on Wolfkin bodies.
- Cultural and trading zones adjacent to shore, rather than every building on steep slopes; occasional foot connections to human hamlets and Brinefolk meeting docks.
- No approved final district politics, specific defense gameplay, owned boats, or named characters.

## 8. Routes, vistas, landmarks

- **Lake Road:** on stable higher ground above marsh margins, connects wolfkin shore, human southern fishing hamlet approach, ferry/contact landings, forest trails and future Greatstone Bridge.
- **Water routes:** conceptual channels from west/northern feeders to central waters and the eastern Brinefolk cove; ferry/boat play not approved.
- **Three recommended vista compositions:** sunlit south shore looking north across islands to wolfkin halls; shaded willow inlet with Brinefolk structures reflected beyond reeds; western high trail overlooking central lake/river outflow.
- **Proposed reveals:** one waterfall entering northern lake, underwater/root shelf, stone bridge over southern outflow, magical spring near east cove. Protect subtle Sylweave and avoid frequent glow.
- **Sound and mood proposals:** water lapping, reed/willow rustle, distant waterfall, insects/birds, soft distant community activity. Fog in dawn coves, clearer mid-day vistas, gentle evening reflection, storm roughening of open water but sheltered reeds remain comparatively calm.

## 9. First implementation sequence and gates

1. **Blocker:** desktop demo acceptance [issue #16](https://github.com/Systemkill90/WildBound/issues/16) before loading any replacement world.
2. **Engineering:** reconcile [512m slice](greenveil-first-corridor-engineering-plan.md) with map: reliable lake waterline and dry overlook, small feeder/outflow, smooth shore gradients.
3. **Surface:** build five shoreline material blends using tested landscape layers, low-cost shore water and vegetation placeholders in isolated test map.
4. **Village blockouts:** reserve separate Wolfkin dry-shore hall site and Brinefolk protected-cove trade platform, not full NPC villages; inspect sightlines and dry paths.
5. **Underwater pass:** build one shallow shelf visual test in engine; no required swimming, boats or fish gameplay.
6. **Visual acceptance:** compare against master artwork and terrain/forest floor specs in different lighting/weather states, inspect shoreline for floating reeds, shoreline seams and overdraw.
7. **Actual playability:** independently launch and traverse a packaged Win64 scene, record player collision, framerate/frame-time, GPU/VRAM and reproducibility; preserve current demo and saves. No automatic merge.

**Status:** document authored/staged. No terrain sculpt, UE import, underwater mesh, NPCs, fish or real gameplay tests created in this pass.
