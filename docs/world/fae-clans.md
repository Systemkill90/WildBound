# Wildbound — The Six Great Fae Clans

**Status:** Six clans and their broad identities, history and ruins **approved by owner October 21, 2026**. Individual quest rewards, exact settlement locations, individual characters and implementation are still proposals. Do not reuse the previously rejected dark-forest naming root in Wildbound.

## Established foundation

The fae were once the most powerful, flourishing and widespread people in the realm, arranged into diverse clans with regional cultures. The plague linked to the Veyrath's chemical poisoning of Ellowyn and the Sylweave devastated them, leaving **many abandoned fae villages** across the world. Fae regenerate wounds rapidly and resist ordinary ailments, but fae-specific disease can kill them; they can die of old age, though the oldest known lived 20,000 years. **Syltouch** is the fae name for their direct root communion.

Encounters with individual fae are now rare. One major remote surviving village gathers people from different clans far from Ellowyn to avoid the plague and learn how to save her. It has not yet discovered a cure. Every fae remains connected to the Sylweave, as do humans, monsters and all other life.

The six clan names and broad specializations are **owner-approved lore**; precise ecological sites remain open. retain diversity within each clan rather than assigning a single personality to an entire people. Fae may intermarry, migrate, form mixed communities, disagree with leaders, or be members of no clan.

## The surviving refuge

**One major surviving multi-clan fae village** is an owner-confirmed plot element; its name and location remain undecided. It was established far from Ellowyn so surviving families and researchers could evade the plague. A proposal: Elarith healers coordinate disease studies; Sylvaren maintain fragile distant Syltouch; Aethrynn investigate human Weir history; Thornwyld support animal rescue; Vaelora manage clean-water access; Veltharyn negotiate secrecy, aid and outsiders' access.

The refuge should be **lived-in**: homes, gardens, craft workshops, animal care, disputes, elders, new generations and grief. Its residents are not only quest dispensers. Whether ancient rivalries threaten their survival is open.

**Owner-approved quest structure:** The detailed history of how each clan fell is primarily optional side-quest content, not mandatory main-story exposition. See [The Six Lost Legacies](quest-framework.md#the-six-lost-legacies--optional-fae-history-questlines) for suggested quest arcs; individual quest titles and rewards remain proposals.

## Abandoned villages as environmental storytelling

Design distinct and recognizable ruins across the world, each with clues to how a community fell: a timely evacuation, a locked quarantine hall, a flooded river court, an abandoned beast sanctuary still inhabited by descendants of its protected animals, a deserted watchtower full of records, or an apparently pristine sealed village. Not every empty site means every resident died. Some may have fled, and a few survivors may remain.

Quest approaches include recovering personal histories and craft knowledge, humane wildlife rehabilitation, restoring individual heritage sites, discovering old root memories through Syltouch, negotiating with living descendants about sacred places, and finding evidence about the disease. Rewards should have homesteading and relationship value, rather than merely weapons.

## The disfavored naming root

The owner dislikes the specific word **“Gloam”** and wants it removed **entirely** from active Wildbound names. Do not propose new place, creature, clan, court or biome names using that root. Where older art references or filenames contain it, preserve historical filenames and artwork until asset renaming and link repair can be performed safely, but remove the word from current player-facing descriptions and drafts. Candidate alternatives for shadowed forest naming: **Moonshade Wood**, **Veltharyn Thicket**, **Mistralwood**, **Silverveil Forest**. None of these alternatives has been approved.

## Implementation boundary

No fae clans, populated fae settlements, plague simulations, questlines, Syltouch system or bestiary content are implemented or tested. These are future content ideas separate from the verified small Greenveil UE5 prototype. A scoped first proof would be one distinct clan-related NPC, one heritage clue and one useful homestead reward, with multiplayer/save validation before expanding.
