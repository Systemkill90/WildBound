# 02 | The Fae diaspora and six clans

## Approved history and biology
Fae are exceptionally long-lived but mortal. The oldest known reached **20,000 years**; ordinary lifespan is not fixed. They regenerate wounds quickly yet succumb to old age and Fae-specific illness. **Syltouch** names their direct communion with Ellowyn's Sylweave through roots. A devastating Sylweave-connected plague emptied numerous villages. One large settlement relocated far from Ellowyn, where the six clans collaborate to survive and seek her restoration. Fae sightings elsewhere are rare.

## Cultural design matrix
All names and broad clan identities below are **approved**. Colors, professions, architecture, customs and game hooks are **proposed**.
| Clan | Approved identity | Proposed visual language | Everyday culture | Possible quest/husbandry connection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sylvaren** | Rootkeepers | Bent living timbers, earthen arches, copper/root motifs | Protect routes of Syltouch, map silent groves | Learn respectful root sampling |
| **Vaelora** | Riverbound | Reed roofs, stone piers, indigo and silver | River routes, flood histories, boat building | Wetland animal care and safe crossings |
| **Aethrynn** | Starwardens | Open observatories, wind bells, deep blue | Records, navigation, long seasonal calendars | Night migrations and wayfinding |
| **Veltharyn** | Veilkeepers | Layered screens, shuttered archive courtyards | Preserve hidden records and dangerous history | Trace missing village archives |
| **Thornwyld** | Beastbound | Barn-canopies, timber corrals, antler/leaf emblems | Wildlife guardianship and bonded animal traditions | Observe, gain trust, design humane pens |
| **Elarith** | Dawnhealers | Daylit terraces, herb courts, white stone | Healing, quarantine, public gardens | Diagnose habitat distress without instant cure |

### Distinguish the clans by *needs*, not just costume
- Sylvaren need uncontaminated paths and access to old roots.
- Vaelora need reliable clean water and functional routes.
- Aethrynn need observation points and cultural memory.
- Veltharyn need secure archives without withholding all exposition.
- Thornwyld need room for animals, migration and responsible husbandry.
- Elarith need isolation spaces, herb plots and access to the sick.

## Remote refuge (name and location **not approved**)
**Concept layout:** a shared village of six walkable precincts with **one common plaza**, an enclosed care garden, quarantine wing, water purification terraces, animal shelter, archive, stargazing platform, and guarded Syltouch chamber. The design should visibly show cooperation *and* tensions over finite supplies. It is not an invulnerable magical utopia.

### Settlement grid (concept only)
| North | Center | South |
|---|---|---|
| Aethrynn sky terrace | Common council & supply hall | Elarith recovery gardens |
| Veltharyn memory vault | Community kitchen and market | Thornwyld animal courtyards |
| Sylvaren enclosed root hall | Shared well and water treatment | Vaelora river terrace |

### Proposed NPC archetypes
- Sylvaren elder who can no longer hear roots, fears admitting it.
- Vaelora engineer who tracks clean water and refuses untested shortcuts.
- Aethrynn apprentice maintaining a sky calendar interrupted by migration.
- Veltharyn archivist who believes truth must be released in careful order.
- Thornwyld keeper who values animal welfare over desperate resource extraction.
- Elarith healer who argues the refuge must accept sick wanderers.
Each is a **character pitch**, not a named approved NPC.

## Syltouch design options (NOT decided)
- Direct contact with living roots rather than generic telepathy.
- Sensory understanding of flow, illness, location or remembered emotions, **not omniscience**.
- Root health, proximity, and individual competence could limit contact.
- Fae disease may endanger contact; who can safely commune remains unresolved.
