## Ecological simulation layering (proposal)
1. **Static habitat facts:** moisture, slope, shelter, vegetation coverage. Cheap; useful before a world simulation.
2. **Local animal needs:** hunger, thirst, stress, sleep, habitat preference. Must be server-authoritative.
3. **Simple bounded ecological reactions:** grazed patches, predator presence, weather shelter effect.
4. **Seasonal/migratory simulation:** future, only once persistent saves and animal limits are reliable.

## Greenveil → March prototype boundary
A short creek becomes murky; meadow birds give way to frogs; low ground softens underfoot, rooflines shift from cottages to stilts. The player should sense the transition before any title card. Prototype in a **small corridor**, not a second full region.

## Magic restraint
The ordinary must remain recognizable; visually strange phenomena should have observational clues and ecological effects rather than omnipresent particles. Protect performance and legibility at night and in fog.

## Naming note
A rejected shadow-forest word appears in older art studies and filenames. New concepts intentionally avoid it. Renaming historical assets must preserve links and does not imply the old name is still accepted.
