## Animal system connection
- **Needs:** nourishment, hydration, safety, rest and weather shelter.
- **Trust:** observation, body-language cues and predictable care; no instant ownership.
- **Care:** feeding and watering must consume supplies and have validated range checks.
- **Breeding:** only after stable persistent animal IDs, parentage and inheritance rules.
- **Enclosures:** navigation and animal size determine practical pen requirements.
- **Offline server time:** define caps, starvation/grief policy and recovery before persistence is shipped.

## Minimal animal roster proposal
1. Brindlefowl or plain farm bird — repeatable feeding, water and enclosure test.
2. Grounded deer / Mosshorn-like prototype — wild stress/trust and grazing.
3. Bog Nibbler **later** — contrast wetland habitat.
This is a prioritized *proposal*, not approval of those species.

## Technical and art production checklist
For each species: turnarounds; consistent measured body size; bones/rig; locomotion set; idle, alarm, feeding and resting; collider; behavior-state map; needs and trust tuning; client replication; persistence; population cap; LOD; audio; documented art rights. Concept art alone satisfies none of these.
