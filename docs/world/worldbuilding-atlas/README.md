## Non-negotiable foundations
- Wildbound is **cooperative survival and homesteading**, not a combat-only RPG; animals and persistent shared homes matter.
- Approved lore: Ellowyn, morally neutral life-sustaining Sylweave, the human-origin Veyrath and Weirs, chemical extraction contamination, recurring hunt for a pure batch, Threnvahl collective, Ellowyn's gift to players, long-lived Fae, Syltouch and six named Fae clans.
- The Fae plague devastates their civilization; a remote multi-clan refuge survives, with widespread abandoned villages.
- **Never reuse the previously rejected shadow-forest naming term** in new names. Legacy artwork and file paths may still contain it pending careful migration.
- Approved premise != designed specifics != implemented features != tested game.

## Production-facing rules
Every concept needs: silhouette and materials, ecological/cultural purpose, player encounter, minimal first test, multiplayer authority/persistence implications, dependencies, and rights/source tracking. **Do not** turn the entire atlas into a milestone or claim designs are final.

## Art guide
The SVG boards are original editable **schematic** concept grids, not painted creature concept art. For illustrated creature sheets, use the existing [20-creature concept atlas](../../art/concept-atlas/README.md), which remains exploratory. Original final production art and rigged game assets still need design and validation.
