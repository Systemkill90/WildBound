# Approved visual reference — Dwarves, Kethri and Nimrui

**Approved by owner: October 22, 2026.** This six-character group portrait is the preferred visual reference for these species during character concept development. The original ChatGPT-generated image was saved as **Wildbound_Approved_Dwarves_Kethri_Nimrui.png** in the conversation; this Markdown document does not itself upload the binary to GitHub.

## Dwarves (foreground, male and female)

- Distinctly short dwarven adults, but not caricature-like or unnaturally tiny.
- Broad, stocky, physically powerful and muscular, **not obese**.
- Pale complexions from living mainly inside mountains; natural freckles, weathered faces, scars and rugged features.
- **Not sexualized or glamorized**. Ordinary, worn, less conventionally attractive facial features; practical full-coverage forge/work and armor outfits.
- Braided hair, a large beard on the male, intricate but weathered mountain-forged armor, fur trim, tools and axes.

## Kethri (upper left, male and female)

- Small **adult** forest people, approximately Nimrui height, not childlike.
- Distinct upright wolf ears and bushy tails, humanlike faces, agile bodies.
- Fur, natural leather, forest clothing, amber-eyed alert expressions.

## Nimrui (upper right, male and female)

- Small **adult** woodland fungal people with dark brown skin.
- Large mushroom-cap headwear with moss, mushrooms and plant details.
- Botanical woodland clothing, warm grounded natural look, not blue skin.

## Continuity notes

This group portrait is the approved visual benchmark for all six individuals.
