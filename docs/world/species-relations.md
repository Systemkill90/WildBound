# Wildbound — Relationships Between Peoples

**Status:** Owner-confirmed broad faction relationships, October 22, 2026. This is **narrative worldbuilding**, not implemented diplomacy, NPC AI, quests, or combat. [Peoples](fantasy-peoples-and-villages.md) · [Fae](fae-clans.md) · [Veyrath and Threnvahl](enemy-faction-concepts.md)

## Children of Ellowyn — the common cause

**Owner-confirmed:** In the great struggle against the enemies threatening Ellowyn, **all peoples ultimately fought together in the past and will fight together again**, setting aside even deep suspicion, historical hatred and isolationism. They are all **children of Ellowyn**, connected through the Sylweave. The alliances and prejudices below shape everyday politics and the difficult road to unity, rather than negating their shared final cause. Do not interpret ordinary human distrust or the Threnvahl–human alliance as an assertion that all human individuals are permanently enemies or incapable of joining the united defense. Whether affected Threnvahl can be freed, rescued or fight alongside the defenders is an unresolved story detail.

## Broad cultural and political relationships

| Peoples / faction | Established relationships and attitudes |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Humans** | Broadly isolationist, keeping to their own communities. Other peoples generally dislike or distrust humans. |
| **Elves, Wildlings, Nimrui, Kethri** | A close, friendly **four-way alliance** with mutual support. Elves and fae know each other but remain suspicious of each other. |
| **Dwarves** | Wary of **all** other peoples; especially hostile toward humans. |
| **Fae and Wolfkin** | A close **alliance**. They broadly respect other peoples **except humans**. Fae and elves can be acquainted while remaining mutually suspicious. |
| **Brinefolk and Merfolk** | Close allies; their communities sometimes intermix. Maintain alliances with **fae** and **elves**, but are generally private and self-contained. |
| **Veshari** | Generally keep to themselves; no wider alliance established. |
| **Sirens / Luresingers** | Feared by **all peoples except fae and Brinefolk**. Lack of fear is not automatic friendship. |
| **Threnvahl** | Hated by all peoples; the owner specifies an **alliance with humans**. Whether this refers to all human societies, particular factions or coerced infected servants is not yet settled. |

## Relationship network in words

- **Friendly woodland alliance:** Elves ↔ Wildlings ↔ Nimrui ↔ Kethri; all four are mutual allies, not only a linear chain.
- **Fae–Wolfkin bond:** Mutual alliance. The fae also maintain an alliance with the Brinefolk and Merfolk, although fae–elf relations remain cautious and suspicious.
- **Aquatic alliance:** Brinefolk ↔ Merfolk (including communities with intermingled membership); both also maintain ties with elves.
- **Relative isolation:** Humans, Veshari and dwarves have strong reasons to remain apart; dwarves have particularly strong antipathy to humans.
- **Fear:** Sirens inspire widespread fear, with the explicit exceptions of fae and Brinefolk.
- **Open hostility:** All peoples hate the Threnvahl, but the owner describes Threnvahl and humans as allied. Since Threnvahl are chemically transformed victims of the human-origin Veyrath and not an independent natural species, the *nature of that alliance* is a worldbuilding question, not permission to rewrite Threnvahl history.

## Narrative guardrails

These describe dominant inter-community attitudes, **not immutable innate traits or mandatory behavior for each individual**. Personal friendships, ethical disagreements, prejudice, empathy, intermarriage, mixed villages, dissenters and defectors remain possible. In particular:
- An individual human need not support the Veyrath or the Threnvahl.
- A transformed Threnvahl individual may be frightened, aware or potentially treatable; this does not contradict the broad public hatred and fear.
- The fae and elves may mistrust one another while maintaining diplomatic contact and even overlapping allies.
- An alliance does not mean a people share the same biology or lose distinct cultures.
- The Sylweave still creates and connects **all life**; political hostilities do not change its universal nature.
