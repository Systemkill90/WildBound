# Wildbound quest framework — design proposal

October 21, 2026. Design only; no implemented NPC quests, rewards, enemy combat or companion battles.

**Owner requirements:** main quests support Ellowyn and the Sylweave; many side quests feature village residents and crafting NPCs with meaningful rewards; bonded/tamed animals help players oppose a hostile faction **not yet named**.

## Craft NPC personal questlines

Smith: repair an heirloom, solve a supply problem and learn distinctive hinges/gates. Carpenter: restore a barn and unlock timber shapes. Herbalist: investigate chemically harmed roots and teach care/restoration. Beastkeeper: patiently rehabilitate wildlife and teach humane species-specific training. Boatwright: restore a ferry and teach watercraft patterns. Weaver: create clothes, banners and companion tack. Each mentor has relationships, routines and a story independent of vending recipes.

## Companion animals in battles

**Owner direction:** taming is a useful path toward fighting the still-unnamed enemy, not only a domestic activity. Bonds earned through time, observation, needs and trust permit roles appropriate to species:

- Predators: defense and interception.
- Large grazers: barriers, protecting camps and moving obstructions.
- Flying animals: scouting and early warning.
- Burrowers: discovering tunnels and extraction pipes.
- Magical creatures: sensing Sylweave disruption.
- Large fantasy saurians: powerful specialized allies with demanding care.

A bond must not make every species a combat pet. Preserve species temperament, stress, safety, retreat and animal care. Support noncombat paths and avoid requiring beloved animals to die or rare genetics to progress the main story.

## Reward principles and scope

Reward useful homestead patterns, plants, decorations, costumes, harnesses, craft techniques, traversal tools, NPC trust, regional access and story discoveries—not only weapons or abstract XP. Side quests should affect the world and not become mandatory checklist grind. Co-op rewards and quest state eventually need server authority, persistence, recovery and ownership permissions.

**First future prototype:** one Greenveil villager, three related interactions, an animal-care objective, one journal entry and one tangible craft reward. Playtest and verify replication/save behavior before building a full quest catalog. All of this remains a proposal, not tested gameplay.
