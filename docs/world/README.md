## Places to imagine

### Greenveil Basin · The first home

Rolling meadows, old orchards, warm timber, grazing animals and creeks. Friendly from a cottage doorstep; wild and sometimes uncanny beyond the garden gate.

**Production reality:** the repository contains a **small compiled-and-packaged test clearing**, not a complete Greenveil region.

### The Drowned March · The place beyond the reeds

Mournful blackwater, half-lost roads, low lanterns and fog that swallows familiar sounds. A biome of atmosphere and mystery, not constant jump scares.

**Production reality:** planned biome, not playable.

### Emberstep Highlands · The open horizon

Wind-torn grasses, open-range animal herds, rocky ridgelines and long ranch buildings. This is a **future candidate region**, not confirmed playable content.

[Read the full biome design notes →](biomes.md)

## A world with inhabitants

The ecology is part of the fantasy. Animals adapt to habitat and season; households and shelters adapt to local weather and materials. Strange horns, altered eyes, fungal symbioses, and the occasional impossible tree are ideas to explore carefully rather than effects everywhere.

[Animals & care](../systems/animals.md) · [Building traditions](../systems/building.md)

### Creative drafts, not canon

The [October checkpoint draft](https://github.com/Systemkill90/WildBound/pull/6) explores original fantasy creature morphology, witchy forest pockets and different villages.
