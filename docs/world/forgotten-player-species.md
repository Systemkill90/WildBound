# The Forgotten Player Species — Ellowyn's Last Creation

**Status:** Owner-approved central premise, October 22, 2026; names, physical design, abilities and history are **exploratory proposals** only. Narrative design, not working character models or playable gameplay.

[Ellowyn and the Sylweave](ellowyn-and-the-sylweave.md) · [Species relationships](species-relations.md) · [Peoples](fantasy-peoples-and-villages.md) · [Decisions](../project/DECISIONS.md)

## Established player-character direction

The playable characters are **not fae**. They belong to a **unique original species, ancient and long forgotten, believed to have been wiped out**. With what little energy Ellowyn has remaining, she **creates or restores living members of this vanished species** as a final hope against the Veyrath's assault on her roots. This reinterprets her earlier Last Gift as a **species-rebirth act**, not simply an empowerment bestowed on existing fae or human wanderers.

This player species must be **stronger than fae in its ultimate capabilities**. However, the intended early game remains survival and homesteading: new player characters need to grow, gather knowledge, earn trust, care for animals, build homes and form alliances. Greater ultimate potential is **not** immediate invulnerability or unlimited magic.

Each cooperative player is an equally valid newly created member, not a secondary companion to one chosen hero. Their individual identities, personalities and choices remain their own.

## Original species names — proposals only

- **Orynth** (OR-inth): concise, ancient, distinctive; suggested favorite.
- **Aelythar** (AY-li-thar): flowing, sacred, older-than-kingdoms quality.
- **Thaloryn** (THAL-or-in): monumental, wild and storied.

**No name is approved yet.** Check possible usage conflicts before public release.

## Proposed species concept (not owner-confirmed)

- An ancestral species whose original members disappeared ages before the rise of the Veyrath, so even ancient fae remember them as folklore.
- Ellowyn retains their living pattern through the Sylweave and fashions new individuals, rather than restoring anyone's prior consciousness or obligating players to repeat history.
- Visual identity could be statuesque, visibly non-fae, with mineral/woodlight details or subtle living-vein patterns rather than fae-style wings and tribal tattoos; exact silhouette, anatomy, height, skin, genders and customization are **undecided**.
- Their bond to the Sylweave could mature from faint sensory awareness into a distinct mode of communion exceeding ordinary fae Syltouch in scope, but the mechanics must not invalidate fae society, animal companionship, co-op, survival or nonmagical approaches.
- Initially, the realm should react to their appearance with awe, suspicion and curiosity. Recognition should be earned through story, not assumed universal at first sight.
- The purpose of their lost original civilization, why it vanished and what Ellowyn sacrificed to recreate it remain open mysteries.

## Design and implementation guardrails

No playable species selection, bespoke rig, anatomy, animations, magic, combat balance, narrative scene, spawn logic or multiplayer character progression exists by virtue of writing this document. First test **one humanoid placeholder** with locomotion, animation and clothing compatibility; prototype growth without skipping the survival loop. Verify in UE5 and with dedicated-server replication before asserting feature completion.

Do not mark fae as playable merely because earlier planning considered them for the player roster. Preserve the existing fae visual specification and their narrative role as a distinct NPC people unless the owner changes it.
