# Greenveil Freshwater Brinefolk — village design draft 01

**Date:** 2026-10-25. **Status:** DETAILED PROPOSAL, awaiting owner review. The presence of freshwater Brinefolk in Greenveil is already approved; the village architecture and arrangement below are **not** newly canonized.

## Approved anchor
Freshwater Brinefolk inhabit Greenveil's Great Lake District. Protect their pale pearl-toned appearance, fins/webbing, and large pink/amethyst eyes. Greenveil is ever-green, photorealistic magical realism, with a single believable freshwater ecosystem. The [underwater visual reference GV-LAKE-UNDERWATER-001](../art/environment/greenveil/REFERENCE-INDEX.md) is owner-approved. `Reedwhisper Cove` is a **working name only**.

## Village layout: a three-layer community (proposal)

**A — Shoreward welcome terrace:** a dry-land path from Lake Road descends gently through meadow grass and willow shade to a raised stone landing. One covered market/meeting platform with broad sightlines, trade baskets, benches, freshwater plants and protective rain shade. Visitors are not required to swim.

**B — Shallow-water neighborhood:** slender but credible load-bearing timber piles on stone-footed substrates, woven reed shade screens, steps down to stable docks, shallow terraces over sand and gravel. Repeated dwelling types with varied veranda heights; accessible bridges must remain broad and structurally plausible, not floating ropes.

**C — Submerged protected shelf:** pale river-stone thresholds, carved existing bedrock niches, timber or stone-built underwater entryways and partially submerged chambers; water occupies them unless separately engineered/approved as air-filled. Light reaches them through clear shallow water and dispersed vegetation. Distinct Brinefolk privacy and transport areas; no assumed land-human underwater traversal.

### Four neighborhood precincts (proposed)
1. **Common Reach** — land contact platform, market canopy, sharing spaces.
2. **Willowsteps** — overwater raised homes, rain shelters, lily channels.
3. **Stillwater Shelf** — submerged entrances and habitable waterside stone pockets.
4. **Springward Sanctuary** — private protected end-cove surrounded by reed gardens and a rare Sylweave-touched spring; **not** an invented source of all Sylweave or an obligatory ritual.

## How it feels to explore
From the lake road, visitors descend into the filtered shade of a giant willow. Sunbeams pick out sand and rounded rocks under brilliantly clear water. Narrow plant-lined shallows reveal the *lower* portion of the village; faint blue-white light traces only some tiny Sylweave-touched plants among otherwise ordinary green leaves. Residents' water paths remain legible through gently waving stems. Crossing the contact bridge opens a view to the upper waterside dwellings, while farther below are private submerged entrances seen through moving caustics. At dusk, the cove darkens naturally; it does not become a neon cave.

## Ecological constraints
Lakeward gradient moves from dry meadow soil through saturated bank, silt/gravel shore shelf, submerged plants, then deeper stony basin. Site in sheltered cove, with low wave fetch and spring-fed flow; keep navigation corridors plant-light but not barren. Respect sediment deposition, load-bearing supports, wind/rain exposure and species-friendly privacy. Deepwater housing would require separate structural and traversal study.
