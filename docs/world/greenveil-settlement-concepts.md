# Greenveil — additional settlement concepts (owner discussion, October 25, 2026)

**Status: PROPOSED / UNDER REVIEW.** Greenveil's Kethri, Nimrui and Wolfkin settlement presence is already approved; this page captures later ideas without prematurely locking details.

## Freshwater Brinefolk
Owner requested **freshwater lake Brinefolk in Greenveil's Great Lake District**. This establishes their presence in the district. Potential stilt/reed platforms, semi-submerged rooms, lake shrines, docks and trade connections are **design proposals**, not established architecture or implemented systems. Species name Brinefolk does not automatically imply seawater-only.

## Humans rejecting Veyrath
Owner suggested **human settlements consisting of people who have left or rejected the hostile human Veyrath faction**. This supports the existing canon that not all humans serve the Veyrath or exploit Sylweave.

**Proposed settlement direction:** humble mixed timber/stone cottages, communal ovens, orchards, farmland, vegetable gardens, workshops, repaired dwellings, and signs of an improvised fresh start. Some settlers might have escaped extraction sites or turned against the Veyrath; preserve variation in histories and motivations. No automatic collective guilt or assumption every human was previously a willing Veyrath member.

**Candidate placement:** an open Heartlands meadow refuge and a Great Lake shoreline trading/fishing hamlet, possibly forming careful relationships with Wolfkin and freshwater Brinefolk. Working term 'Freeholds' is **not approved canon**. No named villages, exact counts, politics, quests or map coordinates have been approved.

**Production:** reserve possible homestead/hamlet footprints during landscape blockout. Do not build or imply completed village meshes/NPCs until scope and design approval; keep the near-term playable Greenveil environment as the active priority and new animal gameplay paused.
