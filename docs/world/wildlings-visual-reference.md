# Approved Wildling Visual Reference

**Approved by owner:** October 22, 2026. **Status:** visual concept approved, not modeled or implemented in UE5.

The latest **bright forest Wildlings concept sheet with deep brown skin, green glowing eyes and white tribal tattoos** is the official visual benchmark. The approved image is in the ChatGPT character-design conversation; this Markdown file does not claim its binary is on GitHub.

## Defining appearance

- **Small adult people**, about the height of a wolf (standing as humanoids); proportions should look mature, not childlike.
- Deep, rich **brown skin**, not tan or pale; distinct **white tribal markings** on faces, shoulders, arms and bodies.
- All have **dark curly hair** and **branching antlers**.
- Expressive, eccentric, nearly glowing **green eyes**.
- Organic forest-crafted clothing and equipment: woven grasses, leaves, bark, feathers, natural fiber, leather and found woodland materials.
- Deep affinity for wild animals, often shown alongside wolves, deer, foxes and owls.
- Natural **chameleon-like camouflage** allows blending into surroundings; the approved primary portrait depicts their **true visible form**. Ability implementation and limits remain unconfirmed.
- Warm **bright daylight** and realistic foliage in the concept sheet keep anatomy and materials legible, including accompanying face portraits, turnarounds and camouflage examples.

## Continuity

When depicting male and female Wildlings in future images, keep these proportions, skin tone, tattoos, antlers, eye glow, dark curls and clothing. The approved image is a *design reference*, not an in-engine rig, texture set, or animation proof.
