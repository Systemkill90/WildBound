## Approved appearance
A paired **male and female human-origin Threnvahl**, formerly human and visibly affected by exposure to the Veyrath's chemical poison. They stand in the same blighted extraction encampment, wearing remnants of dark Veyrath clothing and equipment.

- Human faces remain recognizable, but gaunt, grey, weathered and asymmetrically distorted.
- Grey-ashen cracked skin with sickly green luminescence in fissures and branching veins.
- Jagged green-black crystalline growths protrude from shoulders, arms and back.
- Long, warped limbs, clawlike fingers, unnatural bodily proportions and damaged clothes.
- Desolate damp setting with ruined extraction equipment, green-lit vessels, smoke and old stone structures.
- **Tragic rather than generic zombies:** remnants of personhood and history should be visually legible.
- Male and female counterparts share the same basic mutation language without looking identical.

## Lore guardrails
**Veyrath** is a human-origin faction; **Threnvahl** names profound chemical transformation across original species. This specific image represents **human-origin Threnvahl**, not all Threnvahl or a finalized progression system. The appearance is approved; combat abilities, AI, reversibility and stages remain undecided.

Individual species and encounter designs should be developed separately and require owner approval. No Unreal assets, enemy actors or gameplay tests are evidenced by this reference.
