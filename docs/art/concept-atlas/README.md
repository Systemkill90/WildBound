# Wildbound · Illustrated Concept Atlas

[← GitHub home](../../../README.md) · [Art & atmosphere](../README.md) · [World atlas](../../world/README.md) · [Current development state](../../project/CURRENT-STATE.md)

> **Pre-production ideas, not shipped assets.** These eight generated concept sheets explore 20 candidate creatures, six environment directions, 14 starter tools and two homesteader character presentations. They are image studies only: no rigged character models, creature animations, equipment meshes, new playable biomes, or implemented gameplay are implied. Names and traits are working proposals, not final canon.

Newer character and culture references from the Idea Board are preserved in the [October 23 study](idea-board-2026-10-23/README.md). That study is intentionally separate from this original biome and creature atlas.

The 2026-10-21 illustrations are included as optimized, **original-dimension WebP images** in this folder so the GitHub pages load without adding the full uncompressed PNG masters. Art direction follows believable anatomy, timber-and-stone materials, lived-in clothes, and unusual magic tied to ecology.

## Creatures · Meadow and Woodland (5)

![Mosshorn, Lanternhare, Brindlefowl, Velvet Grazer and Bramble Runner concept-sheet plate](creatures-i-meadows.webp)

**Mosshorn · Lanternhare · Brindlefowl · Velvet Grazer · Bramble Runner**

Grounded forest and farm species, with exaggerated antlers, distinctive eyes, unusual coats, and occasional quiet glow. **None is a confirmed production species.**

## Creatures · Marsh and Wetland (5)

![Reedstrider, Bog Nibbler, Fen Warden, Siltfox and Mireback Grazer concept sheet](creatures-ii-marsh.webp)

**Reedstrider · Bog Nibbler · Fen Warden · Siltfox · Mireback Grazer**

Reed-dwelling waders, root-diggers, wetland grazers and rare guardians. Creature designs should serve movement, feeding and marsh ecology.

## Creatures · Highland and Open Range (5)

![Thistlemaw, Crownmaw, Flint Ram, Duskmane Elk and Tangletail Grazer concept sheet](creatures-iii-highlands.webp)

**Thistlemaw · Crownmaw · Flint Ram · Duskmane Elk · Tangletail Grazer**

Rough terrain, large herds, defensive horn anatomy and cold-weather survival. The species names and roles are exploratory.

## Creatures · Rare and Mythic (5)

![Rootback, Whisperbough, Hollowhorn Boar, Gloamwing Roclet and Moonshade Lizard concept sheet](creatures-iv-mythic.webp)

**Rootback · Whisperbough · Hollowhorn Boar · Gloamwing Roclet · Moonshade Lizard**

Rarer beings with fungal symbiosis, living timber, resonant tusks or reflective feathers. A sentient tree is a long-term idea with substantial AI, rig and ecology costs.

## Production and attribution notes

- These eight files were generated as **Wildbound concept art in the October 21, 2026 design session** and committed at their original canvas dimensions as lossy WebP exports for GitHub browsing.
- The images contain generated small printed labels and speculative biological details. Treat any fine-print inaccuracies as illustration artifacts, **not canonical game-design requirements**.
- Final creature and equipment designs require coherent anatomy, interactions, animation, collision and gameplay proof.
- The eventual male/female character presentation needs a consistent modular clothing system, rigging, animation and accessibility review.
- The current art is separate from third-party Unreal sample licensing. See [Asset credits](../ASSET-CREDITS.md).
- No finished Unreal models or gameplay features were added by this art-only update.
