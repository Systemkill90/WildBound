# Game vision

[Home](../README.md) · [Biomes](world/biomes.md) · [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md)

## The promise

Build a lasting life with other people in a wilderness whose animals, weather, and materials feel believable, while its oldest places feel quietly impossible. Cooperative survival, voxel homesteading, and deep animal relationships form the proposed experience.

## Design pillars

1. **Animals are neighbors and dependents.** Wildlife has needs and boundaries. Tamed animals retain behavior and personality; care matters throughout their lives.
2. **Home is earned through care.** Cottages, gardens, barns, fences, and community spaces support an ongoing life.
3. **The wild remains wild.** Learning habitats, weather, and animal behavior matters. A cozy valley still has predators and consequences.
4. **Magic lives in the environment.** Moths gather at an ancient spring, an orchard blooms out of season, and lights cross still bog water. Familiar natural forms carry subtle impossibilities.
5. **Cooperation builds continuity.** Friends share persistent worlds, homesteads, exploration, and stewardship.

## A proposed day

Check water and bedding, notice a lamb’s inherited coat, mend an enclosure, and share chores with a friend. Track an unfamiliar herbivore beyond the meadow. Return with seeds and a story about a drowned causeway whose lanterns were already lit.

## Rustic realism touched by old-world magic

Muddy boots, timber joints, mossy stone, candlelight, animal breath, creaking barns, and wet reeds establish physical credibility. Environmental magical realism adds wonder and uncertainty through light, sound, ecology, ruins, and folklore. Greenveil offers cottagecore warmth; the March offers ancient loss and unease. Keep these regions original, with their own lore and assets.

## Scope discipline

First prove a small homestead, one animal care loop, voxel building, and two-player persistence. Species, biomes, genetics depth, and platforms expand only after measured results. Combat, economy, seasons, map size, player capacity, and explicit magical abilities remain unresolved. All gameplay in this vision is planned.
