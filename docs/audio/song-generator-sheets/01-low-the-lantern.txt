WILDBOUND — LOW THE LANTERN

STYLE PROMPT (paste into music generator's style/description field)
Slow, haunting but warm marsh-folk work shanty, 52 BPM rolling 6/8, D Dorian. Weathered Brinefolk female or male lead, answered by an intimate communal chorus of 2–4 singers loosely harmonizing; no mechanically perfect choir. Sparse wood taps in time with oars, rope creaks, gentle blackwater lapping, distant fog bells. A believable village singing during dusk net-mending. Deeply wistful and slightly eerie, but full of belonging. Clear ENGLISH SUNG lyrics, actual melodic vocal performance, no spoken narration. No pirate tavern music, pop drums, epic orchestra or modern synth beat.
