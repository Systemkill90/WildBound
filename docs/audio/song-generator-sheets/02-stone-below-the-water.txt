WILDBOUND — STONE BELOW THE WATER
ORIGINAL CONCEPT SONG · GENERATION SHEET · DRAFT

STYLE PROMPT (paste into music generator's style/description field)
Slow ethereal merfolk ancestral hymn, 46 BPM spacious 3/4, E minor with suspended harmonies. A tender low voice joined by overlapping higher and lower female and male voices with gentle layered underwater resonance; sung as a shared memorial and lullaby in a flooded ancient stone hall. Delicate shell chimes, very soft natural drones, glimmers of water movement, almost no percussion. Intimate and ancient, beautiful and faintly uncanny, emotional warmth and reverence. Clear ENGLISH SUNG lyrics, not narration. Avoid glossy fantasy pop, large orchestra or electronic drums.

[Verse 1]
Below the reed, below the rain,
We keep the hall, we keep the flame.

Carry the names. Carry them home.
Here in the deep, no one sings alone.
